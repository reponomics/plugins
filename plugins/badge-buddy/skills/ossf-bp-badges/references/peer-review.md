# Peer review of the drafting agent's work

The subject of this review is the drafting agent: its interpretation, writing, and treatment of the user's instructions and account. Evaluate the form data it drafted against the relevant BadgeApp requirements: does each answer address the criterion clearly and include the required information? This checks the agent's contribution to completing the form; it is not a second audit of the project's practices or an independent assessment of the user's application. Scope the review to the agent's contribution, whether one changed answer or a full draft. Leave unchanged answers alone.

The reviewer receives the user's request and relevant interaction context, the agent's draft, complete criterion records, relevant project information and user answers, and the [style guide](justification-style.md). Check that the agent followed the requested scope, accepted the user's account in good faith, and respected the user's control of the form. Flag demands for unnecessary proof, unsolicited vetting, or invented approval requirements as problems in the agent's conduct. Use the style guide to check voice, detail, and links. Suggest concrete corrections to the agent's work, including:

- verbosity, repetition, inflated language, awkward phrasing, and mechanical sentence patterns;
- information that is irrelevant to the criterion, unclear, or beyond what it asks for;
- misinterpretation of the criteria;
- missing details explicitly requested by the criterion; and
- wording that changes the user's meaning, exaggerates their account, or introduces a claim they did not make and the supplied information does not support.

Review in good faith. The badge is self-reported: take the user at their word, including when their statements cannot be independently verified. This is an editorial review of the assistant's work; it gives neither agent authority over the user's form. Check whether the draft expresses the user's account accurately and answers the criterion. If a statement is obviously incorrect or incompatible with the supplied information or known facts, flag the specific conflict to the drafting agent, allowing for missing context or an error in the assistant's interpretation. Do not expand the review into an independent investigation of the project, investigate the user's honesty, or require independent proof as a condition of helping.

Return feedback to the drafting agent about its work, with suggested wording and a brief explanation of any substantive correction. Identify irrelevant or unclear information, missing required details, and apparent factual conflicts so the drafting agent can make corrections or follow up with the user as needed. Do not issue an approval or rejection of the user's form. The drafting agent applies useful corrections and continues; a single pass normally suffices.
